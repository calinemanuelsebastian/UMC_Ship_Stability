# Step 3: remaining stability data

The educational source remains the supplied booklet. The audit compares the existing starboard KN grid at each original port reference displacement and printed initial trim. All 19 stability-manual cases agree within 0.001121 m; 18 ballast-plan cases differ by up to 0.006692 m. This is evidence for an explicitly estimated symmetric buoyancy extension, not proof of exact symmetry or a regulatory tolerance. Reproduce with `node scripts/audit-port-symmetry.mjs`. The ship-package resource `documentation.port-symmetry-audit` contains residuals, source pages and fingerprints.

In Wind and rolling assessment, choose a profile and expand Derived data — educational estimates. An opt-in checkbox extends the local weather curve toward port. It reflects KN, not GZ: at negative angle, GZ = -KN + (KG + FSM/displacement) sin(abs(angle)) - TCG cos(abs(angle)). It retains zero once, preserves original starboard samples and computes only when inputs/selection change. Original source curves, live 3D attitude and assessment defaults are not changed. Source starboard flooding thresholds are not mirrored; unknown port flooding remains an explicit limitation. A general signed curve for 3D should remain an explicit future mode, not a silent replacement of documented results.

Geometry diagnostics show CB = displacement / density / (1.0028 * LPP * B * moulded draft). The appendage-volume convention remains an assumption, even though the design condition reproduces the source CB approximately. The separate deck-edge estimate atan((depth - draft)/(B/2)) assumes vertical sides and ignores trim, heave and real deck contours. Both remain estimates and neither fills weather inputs automatically. The source design values LWL 123.950 m, B 17.400 m, draft 8.000 m and appendage coefficient 1.0028 were visually checked on manual PDF 34. LPP is 121.700 m; it must not be substituted for LWL in the roll formula.

The existing geometry explicitly labels underwater widths and flare as inferred. General Arrangement and photographs constrain the visible exterior, not validated underwater offsets. It therefore cannot supply reliable condition-specific LWL, immersed volume or flooding limits. Manual PDF 18 was visually checked again: the two open engine-room supply vents at x=1.5, y=±5.515, z=17 m are still accompanied by the red instruction to update against the final Damage Control Plan. Using the booklet as the educational basis does not remove that numerical qualification. No exact downflooding limit is invented from these coordinates or the visual mesh.

The document search inventories the supplied PDF collection with source hashes and relevant text hits in `docs/audits/step3-document-search.json`. This complements visual checks and previous drawing reviews; absence of a text hit does not exclude image-only data. Scripts do not modify supplied documents.
