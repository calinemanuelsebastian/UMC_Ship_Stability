# Current capabilities and limits

The app supports educational loading and numerical stability studies. It is not an approved loading instrument or a declaration of complete IMO compliance.

- Prepare fuel, service liquids and onboard weights; place cargo and support members; adjust ballast and inspect bounded auto-ballast proposals.
- Use cargo/support templates, 2D and 3D editing, scoped undo/redo, tank capacities and liquid-property calculations supplied by the vessel.
- Review weight totals, available hydrostatics, trim, cross-curve-derived stability, applicable intact/weather checks and declared-input securing/support screening. Missing source data remains unresolved.
- Save named conditions/voyage stages locally; transfer editable JSON and ship/template packages; export a snapshot report as JSON and printable HTML. See [backup instructions](backup-and-handover.md).
- The renderer visualizes calculated or explicitly imposed attitudes. Reconstructed geometry does not independently supply verified hydrostatics. Imposed attitude is not a flooding or dynamic-wave simulation.
- Dunnage geometry and density do not establish structural adequacy. Ship-specific securing approval, damage stability, longitudinal strength and pump/transfer sequencing remain outside the demonstrated scope.
- Browser storage is origin/profile-specific. Keep exported backups. Physical-device usability, maximum-scene performance, fresh browser-crash recovery and independent domain acceptance remain open: [P16 evidence](audits/p16-acceptance/assessment.md).

P16 engineering verification is ongoing. No full operational acceptance or deployment approval is implied by passing automated tests.
